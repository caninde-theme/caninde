# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Canindé to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct, in full and by reference. Please read it: it applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any public space where someone represents the project.

In short:

- Be kind and respectful. Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for yourself.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate or harmful.
